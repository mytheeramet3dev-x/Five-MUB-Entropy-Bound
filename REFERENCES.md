# References

## Primary source for the 7-bit value

1. L. Riccardi, C. Macchiavello, and A. Maccone, “Tight entropic uncertainty relations for systems with dimension three to five,” *Physical Review A* **95**, 032109 (2017). DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevA.95.032109](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.95.032109). arXiv: [1701.04304](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.04304).

   Relevant point: the paper reports the five-MUB, dimension-four value 7 bits and equality examples. Its multi-MUB optimization is numerical; this project should not attribute a new universal analytical proof to that paper.

## Experimental context

5. Serino et al., “Experimental verification of tight entropic uncertainty relations in dimensions three to five,” arXiv: [2507.05025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05025) (2025 preprint; later publication information should be checked against the journal record before final citation).

   Experimental validation of reported bounds is not a substitute for a universal mathematical proof.

## Citation policy

- Check final bibliographic metadata (author order, title, volume, pages, DOI, publication date) against publisher or arXiv records before manuscript submission.
- Distinguish theoretical proof, numerical optimization, experimental validation, and formal verification.
- Do not claim that the exact d=4, five-MUB problem is officially listed as open unless a primary authoritative source explicitly establishes that status.
